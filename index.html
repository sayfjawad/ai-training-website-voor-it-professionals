Why AI Matters Now

Learning AI in the right way is no longer optional.

The market is shifting, client expectations are changing, and AI is moving rapidly from experimentation into everyday business operations, products, and decision-making.

The question is no longer whether AI will affect your role, but how prepared you are for that change.

This foundational awareness masterclass provides a practical introduction to AI governance and the key concepts needed to understand, manage, and govern AI effectively.

The First Step

The first step is visibility

What is happening? Where are you today? What capabilities do you have? What risks are emerging? What opportunities are available? And what direction should you take next?

This masterclass provides that foundation.

Target Audience

For professionals who need to understand AI governance but do not need to be AI specialists, including:

Business & Management | Risk | Compliance | Information Security | Data | IT | Legal & Privacy | Internal Audit | Product & AI Teams

What This Masterclass Covers

  1. AI as an Emerging Risk

    Understand why AI creates new risk dimensions and how AI risk differs from traditional technology risk.

  2. AI Challenges & Risk Areas

    Explore key challenges.

  3. AI Objectives & Business Value

    Understand what organisations are trying to achieve with AI and how governance enables AI adoption while controlling risk.

  4. AI Application Types

    Understand the main types of AI applications.

  5. AI Engineering & Building Capabilities

    Understand the key capabilities involved in building and deploying AI.

  6. Core Elements of AI Governance

    Introduction to the main governance components.

  7. Regulatory & Governance Landscape

    Introduction to major AI governance frameworks and standards, including the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, and related AI standards.

Value & Outcomes

After the masterclass, participants will be able to:

  • Understand

    What AI is, how it is being used, and why it creates emerging risks.

  • Recognise

    The main AI risk areas, challenges, and governance requirements.

  • Differentiate

    AI application types, levels of autonomy, and the governance implications of each.

  • Connect the Pieces

    Understand how AI strategy, engineering, risk, security, privacy, compliance, and governance work together.

  • Make Better Decisions

    Use a common AI governance vocabulary and framework when evaluating AI initiatives and risks.

What This Masterclass Is Not

This is not a technical AI engineering masterclass.

It does not focus on coding, model development, technical configuration, platform-specific implementation, or tools that may change rapidly.

Technology will evolve. Platforms will change. New tools will replace old ones. What remains valuable is understanding the direction of travel, the capabilities required, and the risks that must be managed.

The Outcome

Participants leave with a practical foundation for understanding AI governance overall structure, enabling them to engage confidently in AI initiatives, identify key risks, and understand governance responsibilities.
